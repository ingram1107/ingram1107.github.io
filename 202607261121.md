# Active Redundancy

Active [redundancy](202607261118.md) is more common to be deployed in complex
systems in order to achieve [High Availability (HA)](202607261046.md) without
performance decline.

Multiple items of the same kind are incorporated into a design that includes a
method to *detect failure* and automatic reconfigure the system to bypass failed
items using a *voting scheme*. However, this may introduce more complex failure
modes into a system, such as continuous system reconfiguration due to a faulty
voting logic.
