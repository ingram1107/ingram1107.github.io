# Commvault Oracle Database Found RMAN Catalogue Logs on The Disk

One of the common issues in [Cross-Server restore](202607181344.md) is that the
[RMAN catalogue](202607180014.md) logs are found on the disk.

RMAN will be confused and fail to carry out [Data Restore](202608121923.md).
The best solution to this is to remove the files as they have no use after the
restore.
