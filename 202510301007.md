## On the Pursuit of Learning (讚學)

**Title translation note**: 《讚學》's 讚 can be interpreted as 進 (to advance),
and it has the meaning "to encourage". Thus, the title can be understood
as《勸學》, which is a common title among Confucian scholars.

The first sentence
"天地之所貴者人也，聖人之所尚者義也，德義之所成者智也，明智之所求者學問也" and
the second "雖有至聖，不生而知；雖有至材，不生而能" presented the core value of
Confucian, that is the importance of learning. 義 can be understood as the
natural order of one thing, the absolute moral principle, where in itself is the
end not the mean. And only through learning, people can become virtuous (聖人).
Coupled with the examples from other virtuous people (十一君), we should become
a student of someone to learn the way of virtuous path.

**Translation note**: 智 shall be understood as 知 (to know).

In the second paragraph, the author cited from Zhouyi (周易):
"君子以多志前言往行以畜其德", argued that without active nurturing, a talent
cannot become an outstanding one. Therefore, he argued that we have to assist
them with friends with good nature, teach them with teachers (明師) from
university (太學), educate them with 《禮經》 and 《樂經》(cited from 《論語》),
guide them with 《詩經》 and 《尚書》, encourage them with 《周易》, and
enlighten them with 《春秋》.

**Translation note**: "君子以多志前言往行以畜其德" should be understood as
"君子以多(識)前言往行以(蓄)其德"
