# mysqladmin

`mysqladmin` has several useful commands or flag, including `ping`,
`version`, `status`, `processlist`, `kill`, and `--help`

`mysqladmin version` shows the version, OS running, the word type (32-bit or
64-bit), [Unix Socket](202211080912.md), and uptime.

`mysqladmin status` shows the uptime and performance statistic.
